About Me

Dedicated to Restoring Balance & Resilience

Hello, I am Anandu S, Founder of ReGain Behavioural Health and a Clinical Psychologist (A) with a strong foundation in evidence-based psychological assessment and intervention.

I work with adolescents, adults, couples, and families, providing ethical, compassionate, and client-centred mental health care. My areas of clinical focus include anxiety disorders, depression, stress, obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), relationship and marital concerns, emotional regulation, adjustment difficulties, and behavioural challenges.

My therapeutic practice draws from a range of evidence-based approaches, including Cognitive Behaviour Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT), and Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP). I tailor these interventions to your unique needs and goals to help you cultivate resilience, improve psychological well-being, and create meaningful, lasting change.

Whether you are seeking in-person support or online therapy sessions, I am dedicated to providing a safe space for healing and personal growth, offering sessions fluently in Malayalam, English, and Tamil.
